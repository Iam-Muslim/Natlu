WHAT THE APP DOES

Recite Quran is a practice tool for people memorising and reciting the Quran. It
listens through the microphone while the user recites aloud and highlights each
word on screen as it is recognised: green when pronounced correctly, red when a
word is skipped or misread, and yellow when the timing of a Tajweed rule (the
prescribed elongation of Madd, Ghunnah or Shaddah) is outside the accepted
range. Tapping a highlighted word shows the expected duration against the
recited one in milliseconds.

Target audience: students of Quranic memorisation (hifz) and anyone revising
their recitation. This practice normally needs a teacher listening in real
time; the app gives that feedback privately when one is not available. It is a
practice aid, not a religious authority: it reports measured timing and does
not rule on the validity of anyone's recitation.

HOW TO TEST IT

No account, login, or credentials are needed. There is no paid content, no
subscription, no in-app purchase, no advertising, and no user-generated content.

1. Launch the app and allow microphone access when prompted.
2. Pick any surah from the list.
3. Tap the microphone button and recite the verses aloud.
4. Words highlight as you recite: green correct, red skipped or misread,
   yellow a Tajweed timing error. Tap a yellow or red word to see the detail.

IMPORTANT FOR THE REVIEWER: the app only responds to recited Quranic Arabic —
the acoustic model is trained on that alone and will not react to other speech
or other languages. If the reviewer does not recite Arabic, the app can still be
tested fully by playing any recording of the chosen verses aloud from a second
device held near the iPhone. The app responds to that audio exactly as it does
to a live voice, and the highlighting can be observed as it happens.

EXTERNAL SERVICES USED

None. The app is entirely self-contained and works with the device offline or in
airplane mode.

- Speech recognition runs on device: a Zipformer CTC acoustic model bundled in
  the app, executed locally through ONNX Runtime (CoreML on iOS).
- No server, API, data provider, authentication service, payment processor or
  third-party AI service. The app contains no networking code at all, and no
  analytics or advertising SDK.
- Recitation audio is processed in memory, never written to disk, and never
  leaves the device.

REGIONAL DIFFERENCES

None. The app behaves identically everywhere. The interface follows the device
language (Arabic or English); no feature or content varies by country.

THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL AND AUTHORISATION

The app bundles three pieces of third-party material, all used within their
licences, and all of which permit free distribution:

1. Quranic typeface — KFGQPC Hafs Smart, King Fahd Glorious Quran Printing
   Complex. Its end-user licence states: "Permission is hereby granted, Free of
   Cost, to any person obtaining a copy of this Font accompanying this license,
   the rights to Use, Copy, Distribute", conditional on the font not being sold,
   modified or reverse engineered. The font is embedded unmodified, and the app
   is free, so both conditions are met.

2. Acoustic model — Quran-Lab zipformer_p-arabic-v3, released under the
   Quran-Lab No-Profit License 1.2. That licence permits use only in software
   that is free to end users and never monetised. This app is free with no
   adverts and no in-app purchases, which is what the licence requires.

3. Quranic text and phonetic index — derived from the open-source
   quran-transcript project and the Quranic Universal Aligner, both credited in
   the app's public source repository. The recited text is the Quran itself.

The app is open source; its licence likewise forbids charging for it in any
form. Published at github.com/Iam-Muslim/Natlu.

PERMISSIONS

Microphone only, requested when the user first starts a recitation session and
used solely to recognise the words recited. Nothing else is requested, and no
data is collected.
