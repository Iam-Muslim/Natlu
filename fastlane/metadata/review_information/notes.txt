The app runs entirely on device. There is no account, no sign-in and no network
connection required; recitation audio is never transmitted or stored.

To test: allow microphone access when prompted, pick any surah, tap the
microphone and recite aloud. Words turn green as they are recognised, red when
skipped or mispronounced, and yellow on a Tajweed timing error. Tapping a
highlighted word shows the expected versus recited duration in milliseconds.

Recitation must be in Arabic; the acoustic model is trained on Quranic Arabic
only and will not respond to other speech.
