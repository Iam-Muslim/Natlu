WHAT THE APP DOES

Recite Quran is a practice tool for people memorising and reciting the Quran. It listens through the microphone while the user recites aloud and highlights each word on screen as it is recognised: green when pronounced correctly, red when a word is skipped or misread, and yellow when Tajweed timing (Madd, Ghunnah, Shaddah) is outside the accepted range. Tapping a word shows expected vs. recited duration in milliseconds.

Target audience: students of Quranic memorisation (hifz) and anyone revising recitation. It is a private practice aid, not a religious authority, reporting measured timing without ruling on recitation validity.

HOW TO TEST IT

No account, login, or credentials needed. No paid content, subscriptions, in-app purchases, ads, or user-generated content.

1. Launch app and allow microphone access.
2. Select any surah from the list.
3. Tap the microphone button and recite verses aloud.
4. Words highlight in real time: green (correct), red (skipped/misread), yellow (Tajweed timing). Tap yellow/red words for details.

NOTE FOR REVIEWER: The app only responds to recited Quranic Arabic (the on-device model is trained exclusively on it). If you do not recite Arabic, you can test it completely by playing a recording of the chosen verses aloud from a second device held near the iPhone.

EXTERNAL SERVICES USED

- Recitation Audio: Public domain verse recordings (Mahmoud Khalil Al-Husary) streamed/cached on-demand from everyayah.com for playback.
- Speech recognition (100% on-device): Zipformer CTC acoustic model bundled locally via ONNX Runtime (CoreML). No audio ever leaves the device.
- Privacy: No user tracking, no authentication, no user accounts, no analytics or ads SDKs. Microphone audio is processed strictly in memory, never written to disk or transmitted over the network.

REGIONAL DIFFERENCES

None. Identical globally. UI follows device language (Arabic or English).
