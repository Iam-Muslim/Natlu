Improve your Quran recitation and memorisation.

Whether you are memorising a new surah or revising your daily portion, the app listens as you recite and highlights each word on screen in real time.

It is built on modern on-device AI and runs entirely on your iPhone. No internet connection is needed, so your privacy is complete and you can revise anywhere.

KEY FEATURES

• Real-time word tracking
Each word turns green when you pronounce it correctly, and red when it is skipped or misread, so you can correct yourself immediately.

• Tajweed and Madd checking
The app measures the length of your Madd, Ghunnah and Shaddah and marks a word yellow when the timing is off. Tap any word to see the expected duration against the one you recited, in milliseconds.

• Voice ayah search
Recite any verse aloud and the app jumps straight to it. Nothing to type.

• Hifz revision and hide mode
Words reveal themselves only as you recite them correctly, so you can test how firmly you have memorised a passage.

• Fully offline, completely private
Your voice never leaves your device. Everything is processed locally: no account, no tracking, no data usage.

• Smart auto-scroll
The page follows the pace of your recitation, so you can recite without touching the screen.

• Adjustable display
Control font size and weight, and pick the theme that reads most comfortably.

The app is entirely free: no adverts, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases.
